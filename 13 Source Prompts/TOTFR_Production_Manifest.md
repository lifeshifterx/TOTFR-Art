# TOTFR Production Manifest

This manifest is the source of truth for the art-production phase before Notion implementation.

## Completion rule
An asset is complete only when its individual final binary exists on the `development` branch and is verified. Local renders, previews, contact sheets, temporary transfer chunks, and staging files do not count.
